## 3. How we keep it fresh

- Batches should arrive at **4 °C or colder**. We keep chilled fish at **−1 °C to 4 °C** and frozen fish at
  **−18 °C or colder**. Readings outside these ranges are flagged to the store team.
- Fish past its best-before time is removed from sale automatically. We pack the batch with the earliest best-before
  time first.
- Your order is cleaned and cut to order, packed, and its temperature is recorded when it leaves the store; a reading
  above 4 °C is flagged.

## 4. Allergens

Fish, crustaceans (such as prawns, crabs and lobster) and molluscs (such as squid, mussels and clams) are common
allergens. Marinated and ready-to-cook items may contain other ingredients; these are listed on the product page.
If you have a food allergy, please check the product page and contact us before ordering if you are unsure. We
prepare different seafood on the same premises, so we cannot rule out cross-contact.

**Check with lawyer:** allergen declaration requirements under the FSS (Labelling and Display) Regulations 2020 for
food sold online and for pre-packed marinated items, and whether the product page must show the full label
information before purchase.

- Do not refreeze seafood that has thawed.
