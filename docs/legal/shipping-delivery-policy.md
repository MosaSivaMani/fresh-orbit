# Shipping and delivery policy

Last updated: {{OWNER: date of publication}}

We deliver fresh fish and seafood from our own stores to your door, the same day, in a delivery slot you choose. We do
not use courier companies and we do not ship by post.

## 1. Where we deliver

- We deliver only to the **pincodes** we serve in each city. Enter your pincode on the website or app to check.
- Each served pincode belongs to one **delivery area**, and each area is served by one of our stores:
  {{OWNER: store names and addresses, or a link to a live list}}.
- If your pincode is not served, you can still browse, but you cannot place an order for that address.
- Prices and what is for sale can differ between stores. You see the prices for your pincode.
- If we pause deliveries in a city, you cannot place new orders there; orders already placed are still delivered.

## 2. Delivery slots

- At checkout you choose a **delivery slot**: a window of time on a given day, shown in local time. Slots are offered
  for the **next 7 days**.
- Each slot has an **order cut-off time**, after which it is no longer offered (for example, the evening before an
  early-morning slot). Slot times and cut-offs are set by each store *(setting)*.
- Each slot takes a limited number of orders. When it is full, choose another slot.
- On days a store is closed (for example holidays), no slots are offered. If we have to close on a day you have
  already booked, we contact you to move or cancel your order; a cancelled order is not charged.

## 3. Delivery fee and minimum order

These are set for each delivery area *(setting)* and are shown in your cart before you order:

- a **minimum order value**, counted after any coupon discount;
- a **delivery fee**; and
- in some areas, **free delivery** above a set order value.

The delivery fee is shown on your invoice. It does not change if the final weight changes.

## 4. Paying on delivery

At launch you pay **on delivery**, in cash or by **UPI** (scan the rider's QR code). Online payment will be added
later.

- Pay-on-delivery is available for orders whose "most you could pay" total is up to **₹5,000** *(setting)*.
- You pay the **final amount** after weighing, which the app shows once your order is packed. See §2 of the
  [Refund and cancellation policy](refund-cancellation-policy.md).

## 5. On the day

1. The app tells you when we start preparing your order and when it is packed, with the final amount (as a
   notification in the app, and on your phone if you allow app notifications).
2. When the rider leaves, you get an SMS with the rider's name and phone number and a **4-digit delivery code**. The
   code is also shown in your order screen while the order is out for delivery.
3. **Give the code to the rider only when you have your order in hand.** The rider cannot complete the delivery
   without it.
4. Please check the package at the door and put the fish in the refrigerator straight away.
5. Your GST invoice is issued when the order is delivered. You can download it from the order screen.

## 6. If we cannot deliver

If nobody is available, the address cannot be found, or the order is refused, the rider records a failed attempt and
we contact you. We can try again **the same day**, up to **3 attempts** in total *(setting)*. Because seafood is
perishable, we do not deliver it on a later day; after the last failed attempt the order comes back to the store.

- **Paid on delivery:** you are not charged.
- **Paid online (when we offer it):** see §6 of the [Refund and cancellation policy](refund-cancellation-policy.md).

## 7. Keeping your fish cold (cold chain)

- Our stores can sell only once they have **cold storage** and an **FSSAI licence** recorded in our system; a store
  without them cannot be switched on.
- We record the temperature of every batch when it arrives (it should be **4 °C or colder**) and keep chilled fish at
  **−1 °C to 4 °C** and frozen fish at **−18 °C or colder**. Readings outside these ranges are flagged to the store
  team.
- Fish past its best-before time is removed from sale automatically, and we pack the stock with the earliest
  best-before time first.
- At packing we record whether a **cold box** was used, and when the order leaves the store we record its
  **temperature**. A reading above 4 °C is flagged for the store team.
