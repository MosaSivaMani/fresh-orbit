# Grievance redressal policy

Last updated: {{OWNER: date of publication}}

We want to put things right quickly. This page tells you how to raise a problem with an order, with your account or
with your personal data, who handles it, and how long it takes.

## 1. Start here: report a problem with an order

For a problem with a delivered order (missing, damaged, poor quality, wrong item or late), use **"Report a problem"**
on the order screen within 24 hours of delivery, with photos. This is the fastest way: it goes straight to our team,
and you can follow it in the app. See the [Refund and cancellation policy](refund-cancellation-policy.md), §4.

## 4. If you are still not satisfied

You can, at any time:

- call the **National Consumer Helpline** ({{OWNER: confirm current number, believed to be 1915}}) or use its online
  portal;
- file a consumer complaint with the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission where you live or work, including
  online through the e-Daakhil portal; or
- for personal data, complain to the **Data Protection Board of India**, after first using our grievance process as
  the DPDP Act s.13(3) requires.

**Check with lawyer:** the helpline number and portal names at publication time.

## 5. What we do with complaints

- Every complaint raised in the app is reviewed by a team member, who approves or rejects it and records why.
- Refunds for approved complaints are approved by a second person before they are paid.
- We use complaints to fix problems in our stores and delivery, and we never penalise a customer for complaining in
  good faith.
