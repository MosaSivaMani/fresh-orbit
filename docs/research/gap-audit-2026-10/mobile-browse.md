# Gap audit: mobile browse screens (October 2026)

Scope: the Flutter app's home (after D292–D293), Shop, search, product page (sizes, cuts, price quote, gallery) and
Saved (wishlist): `apps/mobile/lib/features/catalog/`, `state/catalog_state.dart`, `widgets/commerce.dart`,
`widgets/bold.dart`, `packages/flutter/fresh_theme`, and the endpoints they call in `backend/src/Modules/Catalog`,
`Cart` (price quote), `Customers` (wishlist) and the API host's rate limits. Read-only audit on main `5e0907d`:
nothing was built or run (CLAUDE.md rule 18). Every row was checked in the code; sizes for text scale were worked
out from the fixed layout numbers in the code, not measured on a device.

## Summary

- **No launch Blocker found** in browse. The earlier audits' browse items are still fixed: search limits (D235),
  Change pincode on pincode refusals (D236), sort without `q` (PR #27), freshness, serving, offer line and cold
  storage claims (D239, D240, D274), gallery (D266, D276), Notify me. Their open items still open are the
  `defaultVariant` and `discountBp` fields that the app never reads (`design/mobile.md` §8). These are MB-2 and MB-27 below.
- **Biggest risks:** (1) the server sends a product's sizes in no fixed order, and the app opens the page on, and
  quick-adds, whichever comes first. So a card's "from" price may not be the size that ADD puts in the cart, and
  the page can open on a sold-out size while other sizes are in stock. (2) The app has no Android or iOS deep links,
  so `/product/:slug` links from the website, SMS or WhatsApp never open the app. (3) Browse and search share a
  limit of 60 requests a minute per IP, and in India many phones share one public IP (mobile carrier NAT). (4) At
  text scale 1.5 to 2.0 the product cards, the cut tiles and the Shop's fixed-height rows overflow. (5) Images have
  no disk cache and are decoded at full size.
- Tests are missing for Shop (no test file at all), load-more, Saved with items, the product page's error, 404 and
  not-sold states, and any browse screen except home at 360 × 640 or large text.
- Counts: gaps 0 Blocker, 9 High, 25 Medium, 36 Low. Missing features: 10 Must, 14 Should, 9 Nice.
