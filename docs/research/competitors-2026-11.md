# How similar companies are doing (2026-11-09)

The owner asked how other online fish and meat sellers are doing. The answer set the four business decisions D377–D380.
The figures come from press reports of company filings, not from the filings themselves; sources disagree slightly
on some of them.

| Company | Model | Latest year | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Licious (largest) | Own app, quick-commerce apps, 50+ stores | FY26 revenue ₹1,166 Cr (+47 %); FY25 ₹795 Cr | Still losing money. Cash burn was about ₹187 Cr in FY26, up from ₹168 Cr in FY25, driven by stores and infrastructure. The FY25 net loss was ₹218 Cr. It targets ₹1,800 Cr in FY27 and an IPO. |
| FreshToHome (fish, South India) | App | FY25 revenue ₹421 Cr (+14 %) | Losing about ₹146 Cr a year, roughly flat. The cost of goods (₹481 Cr) is higher than revenue, and it now raises debt rounds. |
| TenderCuts (Chennai) | Stores and online | About ₹5.6 Cr a month (Aug 2025) | 10 % store-level EBITDA and 1 % company-level EBITDA. It says it was the first in the sector to make a company-level profit, and it grows on debt. |
| Zappfresh (Delhi) | Online, one region, lean | About ₹130 Cr (FY25, reported) | Profitable, about ₹9 Cr. It is listed on the BSE SME board. |
| Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart | 10-minute delivery | Meat is one category among many | They sell Licious and their own labels. Zepto's own meat label struggled, and the head of its meat business left. |

## What it means for us

1. Losses come from spending ahead of the numbers: marketing, many cities, stores opened too early. We should grow
   one area at a time and only when that area already pays.
2. The companies that make a profit are regional and frugal (TenderCuts, Zappfresh). Their costs are variable, and
   they grow on profit or debt rather than on burn.
3. Buying cost decides the result. FreshToHome pays more for its fish than it earns from selling it. We should buy
   against real orders and price with every cost included.
4. Speed does not win this category. People plan fish purchases and buy from a brand or seller they trust (Technopak
   on Zepto's Relish). Freshness, honest weight (D347) and a fixed slot compete with 10-minute delivery.
5. The benchmark is TenderCuts' 10 % store-level EBITDA. We should reach it before opening a second area.
