# Delivery policy

## Where we deliver

We deliver only to the pincodes listed for each city. Enter your pincode on the site or app to check. If your pincode
is not served yet, you can still browse, but you cannot place an order for that address.

## Delivery slots

- You choose a **delivery slot** at checkout (for example 7–9 am, 11 am–1 pm, 5–7 pm). Available slots are shown for
  the next 7 days. *Configurable per store.*
- Each slot has an **order cut-off time** (for example, the evening before for early-morning slots, or two hours
  before the slot starts). After the cut-off the slot is no longer offered.
- Each slot has a limited number of orders. When it is full, choose another slot.
- On days the store is closed (holidays, maintenance), no slots are offered. If we must close on a day you have
  already booked, we will contact you to move or cancel your order.

## Delivery fee and minimum order

*Configurable per delivery area.*

- Each area has a **minimum order value** (after any coupon discount) and a **delivery fee**.
- Delivery is **free above** a set order value in most areas. The fee, the minimum and the free-delivery value for
  your address are shown in the cart before you pay.
- The delivery fee includes GST where applicable and is shown on your invoice.

## Payment on delivery

At launch you can pay **cash on delivery** or **UPI on delivery** (scan the rider's QR code). Online prepayment will
be added later.

- Cash or UPI on delivery is available for orders up to **₹5,000** (estimated maximum total). *Configurable.*
- You pay the **final amount** after weighing (see "Weight and final price" below), shown in the app before the rider
  arrives.

## Weight and final price

Fish and shellfish are sold by **net weight after cleaning and cutting**. Your order shows an estimate; the final
price is set when we weigh and pack:

- If we pack **more** than you ordered, you pay for the actual weight, but never for more than **10 % above** the
  weight you ordered. *Configurable.*
- If we pack **less**, you pay only for what we packed.
- Items sold by the piece or pack are charged per piece or pack.

## On the day

1. You get a notification when your order is being prepared and when it is packed, with the final amount.
2. When the rider leaves, you get a notification with the rider's name and phone number and a **4-digit delivery
   code**. The code is shown only in your order screen while the order is out for delivery.
3. **Give the code to the rider only when you receive your order.** The rider cannot complete the delivery without it.
4. Please check the package on delivery and refrigerate it immediately.

## If we cannot deliver

If no one is available, the address cannot be found, or the order is refused, the rider records a failed attempt. We
can try again **the same day**, up to **3 attempts** in total. *Configurable.* Because seafood is perishable, we do
not redeliver on a later day; after the last failed attempt the order is returned to the store.

- **Cash / UPI on delivery:** you are not charged for a returned order.
- **Prepaid orders (when available):** a returned order is refunded in full to the original payment method.

Repeated refusals of cash-on-delivery orders may lead us to restrict cash on delivery for the account.
