# Frequently asked questions

## Ordering

**Do I need an account?**
You can browse and fill a cart without logging in. To place an order, log in with your mobile number and the one-time
password (OTP) we send by SMS. Your cart is kept when you log in.

**How do I know if you deliver to me?**
Enter your pincode. If it is served, you see prices and delivery slots for your area. If not, we do not deliver there
yet.

**Why does the price say "per kg" but my cart shows a different amount?**
Fish is priced per kg of **net weight after cleaning and cutting**. The cart multiplies that price by the weight you
chose and adds any cutting charge for the preparation you picked (shown next to each option).

**What do "curry cut", "steaks" and "fillet" mean?**
They are the ways we prepare the fish. Each product lists the options it offers, for example *Cleaned, whole*,
*Curry cut* (bone-in pieces of 3–4 cm), *Steaks* (about 1 cm slices with the centre bone), *Fillet* (boneless). The
weight you order is the weight **after** preparation.

**Why is the final amount different from the order amount?**
Fish cannot be cut to an exact weight. We weigh your order after cutting and charge the actual weight, never more
than 10 % above what you ordered *(configurable)*. If we pack less, you pay less. You see the final amount in the app
before delivery.

**What if an item is not available?**
At checkout you choose: *Call me* (we propose a substitute and you decide within 15 minutes), *Choose a similar item*
(only if it does not cost more), or *Don't substitute* (the item is removed and not charged).

**Can I add instructions?**
Yes, add a note at checkout (up to 500 characters), for example "keep the head" or "call before arriving". Notes are
read by our team but cannot change prices.

## Delivery

**When will my order arrive?**
In the slot you chose at checkout. Each slot has an order cut-off time; slots for the next 7 days are shown.

**How much is delivery?**
It depends on your delivery area and is shown in the cart. Most areas have free delivery above a set order value and a
minimum order value. *Configurable.*

**What is the 4-digit delivery code?**
When your order is out for delivery, the app shows a 4-digit code. Tell it to the rider **only after you have received
your order**; the rider needs it to complete the delivery.

**What if I miss the delivery?**
The rider can try again the same day (up to 3 attempts in total). Seafood is perishable, so we do not redeliver on
another day; the order is returned and you are not charged (cash/UPI on delivery).

## Payment

**How can I pay?**
Cash on delivery or UPI on delivery (scan the rider's QR code). Online prepayment will come later.

**Is there a limit for cash on delivery?**
Yes, ₹5,000 per order (the estimated maximum total) *(configurable)*.

**Are prices inclusive of GST?**
Yes. All prices include applicable taxes; your invoice shows the tax included.

**When do I get an invoice?**
A GST invoice is issued when the order is delivered, for the final amount. You can download it from the order screen.

## Cancellations, complaints and refunds

**Can I cancel?**
Yes, until your order is being prepared (the app shows a Cancel button only while it is allowed). After that, call
support.

**Something is wrong with my order. What do I do?**
Use "Report a problem" on the order screen **within 24 hours of delivery**, choose the item and add photos.

## Coupons

**How do I use a coupon?**
Apply it in the cart. One coupon per order. Some coupons are for the first order only, for certain categories, or need
a minimum order value; the cart tells you why a coupon does not apply.

**I cancelled an order with a coupon. Can I use it again?**
Yes, the coupon use is returned when an order is cancelled (subject to its validity dates and limits).

## Freshness and storage

**How should I store my seafood?**
Refrigerate it as soon as it arrives (0–4 °C) and cook it within 24 hours, or freeze it at −18 °C or below. Each
product page has its own storage note. Frozen products must stay frozen; do not refreeze after thawing.

**Is the fish wild or farmed?**
Each product page says *Wild-caught* or *Farmed* and the region it comes from.

## Account and privacy

**How do I delete my account?**
From your profile, choose "Delete account". You cannot delete it while an order is still in progress. Your personal
details are removed; order and invoice records are kept as the law requires (see the privacy policy).

**How do I stop offer messages?**
Turn off offers in notification settings. Order updates by SMS cannot be turned off because they carry your delivery
code and payment information.
