## 6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app. Deletion is refused while an order is in progress. When you delete it, we
clear your name, email and phone number, deactivate and blank your addresses, remove your wishlist, revoke your
sessions and turn off marketing. Order and invoice records stay, without your contact details where the law allows,
because we must keep them.

## 7. Your rights

Subject to law, you can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, nominate someone to act
for you, and raise a grievance with us. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Data
Protection Board of India.

## 8. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), hash passwords and one-time passwords, limit staff access by role and store, and
record staff actions in an audit log. No system is completely secure; tell us at once if you suspect misuse of your
account.
