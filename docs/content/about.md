## How we handle it

- **Temperature on arrival.** We measure the temperature of every delivery we receive. Fresh seafood should arrive at
  4 °C or colder; anything warmer is recorded and checked before it is sold.
- **Cold storage.** Fresh seafood is kept in a chiller between −1 °C and 4 °C on ice; frozen products in a freezer at
  −18 °C or colder. Temperatures are logged several times a day.
- **First to expire, first out.** Stock is picked by best-before time, so older stock is never held back while newer
  stock is sold.
- **Cut to order.** Fish is cleaned and cut when your order is prepared, not in advance (pre-packed products such as
  fillet packs are marked as packs).
- **Packed cold.** Orders travel in an insulated box with ice gel packs [Owner: confirm packaging]. The packing
  temperature is recorded when the order leaves the store.

## Honest weights and prices

You pay for the **net weight after cleaning and cutting**, not the weight of the whole fish. Fish are natural, so the
packed weight can differ slightly from what you ordered: you never pay for more than 10 % above the weight you
ordered, and if we pack less, you pay less. The full rule is in our
[cancellation and refund policy](cancellation-refund-policy.md).
