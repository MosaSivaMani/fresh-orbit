# One-month plan: 9 November – 6 December 2026

Written 2026-11-08 by the lead. **Status: proposed. The owner confirms the goal, the Must picks (§4) and the
owner track (§3).** Sources: gap audit (`research/gap-audit-2026-10/`, 581 gaps, 232 missing features),
`team/go-live.md`, `team/next-steps.md`.

## 1. Goal for the month

By **6 December**, Fresh Orbitt is **ready for a soft launch**: one store, one city, cash on delivery (COD
launch), real orders from friends and family on the real server, with a GST invoice, SMS sign-in, e-mail
and push working, backups offsite, and the Android app in Play closed testing.

Public launch follows when the owner's licences, Play review and lawyer review are through (§6).

Everything the code needs can be done in the month. What decides the date is the **owner track** (§3):
domain, DLT, FSSAI, GSTIN, Play account and lawyer review have waiting times outside our control. Under D208
these were on hold; **this plan asks the owner to start them in week 1.**

The business side (profit, wastage, buying, customers, cash risk) and the six-month roadmap are in
`research/business-readiness-2026-11.md`; its **P1** items are part of this month.

## 6. What could move the dates

| Risk | Effect | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| DLT approval takes longer than 2 weeks | No SMS sign-in on the real server | Keep the soft launch invite-only; rehearse with the demo; do not add an unapproved fall-back (rule 7) |
| FSSAI licence type needs the state licence | Store cannot be activated | Apply in week 1; registration first if the turnover allows |
| Personal Play account | 14-day closed test before production | Start closed testing in week 3; public app after mid-December |
| Lawyer review late | Policies not final | Soft launch stays invite-only and `noindex` |
| CA says bill of supply for exempt fish | Invoice format change | Invoice template is data; change in a day once the answer is in |
| Too many Medium/Low items | Not all fit in a month | Buy path first; the rest roll into December, per screen |
