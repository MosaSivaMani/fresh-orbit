The website, in Version B

The same look as the app, laid out for a laptop screen: the bottom bar becomes a top header with pincode, search and cart; screens become wide colour blocks with the circle characters; the shop gets a filter side panel and a four-column grid; cart and checkout sit side by side with the bill. On a phone the website looks like the app screens.

Prices are sample values. Fish shapes stand in for product photos.
